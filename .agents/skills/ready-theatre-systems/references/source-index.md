# RTS source index and provenance

## Authority and audit scope

This skill was assembled on 2026-07-13 from:

- the maintained [Ready Theatre Systems Knowledge Base](https://helpdesk.rts-solutions.com/portal/en/kb/ready-theatre-systems);
- the retired [ReadyTicket wiki](http://wiki.readyticket.net/mw/index.php/Main_Page);
- read-only inspection of the `fgb` repository and its checked-in normalized payload examples.

The public crawl found 84 current knowledge-base categories and 84 published article bodies. It also enumerated 792 namespace-zero legacy wiki pages; 195 contained non-empty content and 11 were redirects. The legacy wiki banner states that it is no longer maintained and directs readers to the knowledge base.

The current category tree includes `Software Change List` subcategories for 2015–2026, but the public category/article APIs exposed no published article bodies beneath them at audit time. Recheck the live portal when version history matters.

## Recency rule

For a future task, search the current knowledge base first. If a needed maintained article is newer than this skill or contradicts it, follow the article and update the skill reference. Do not preserve stale instructions for compatibility unless the installed RTS version is known to require them.
